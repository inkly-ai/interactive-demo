What to look for

  • The inline frame fills its box edge to edge — no scrollbars, no grey bands.
  • Resize the window: the frame keeps the demo's ratio and stays under 80% of the viewport height.
  • The pop-up overlay traps the scroll behind it and restores it on close.
  • Clicking through the demo logs step_view / complete events on the left.
  • A form widget with submitTo pointed here shows up on the right.
