1 — Inline embed

The iframe snippet interactive-demo embed --mode inline prints. The frame should show the player alone: no page bar, no canvas, transparent background, and no letterboxing at any window width.

2 — Pop-up embed

The loader snippet: embed.js plus a button that calls InteractiveDemo.open(). The overlay should take the demo's own aspect ratio, close on Escape, and close on a click outside the frame.

3 — Relayed events

Every runtime event the framed player posts to this page.

4 — Form submissions

What this server received at /api/form. Point a form widget's submitTo at it to test the round trip.

What to look for

  • The inline frame fills its box edge to edge — no scrollbars, no grey bands.
  • Resize the window: the frame keeps the demo's ratio and stays under 80% of the viewport height.
  • The pop-up overlay traps the scroll behind it and restores it on close.
  • Clicking through the demo logs step / complete events on the left.
  • Submitting a form widget with submitTo set to this server appears on the right.
